> **HABACOIN PLATFORM TERMS AND CONDITIONS**

> **1. Agreement to Terms**
> By accessing or using the HabaCoin Progressive Web Application ("the App"), you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. The platform is operated as a decentralized utility layer. If you do not agree, you must immediately halt all use of the platform.

> **2. Nature of the Protocol**
> HabaCoin provides non-custodial or managed software interfaces interacting with the Polygon blockchain. The user acknowledges that cryptographic transactions are irreversible. HabaCoin does not retain the capacity to reverse on-chain ledger states.
> 
> **3. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Fraud Prevention**
> * To maintain system safety, the platform reserves the right to implement off-chain transaction risk scoring via our Neon DB infrastructure.
> * Users are strictly prohibited from engaging in structured capital washing, terrorist financing simulation, or obfuscating illicitly obtained funds through the P2P transfer mechanism.
> * Accounts linked to verified fraudulent mobile transactions or malicious social engineering attempts will be blacklisted from utilizing our Relayer nodes and application endpoints.
> 
> **4. Fees and Network Costs**
> While the HabaCoin ecosystem abstracts network fees through gasless relayers to ensure near-zero transaction friction for standard users, the platform reserves the right to reintroduce scalable micro-tariffs to counter systemic DDoS or transaction-flooding network attacks.
